A practical, relaxed approach

Whether you’ve never picked up an instrument before or you’re looking to take your playing to the next level, my teaching is built around real-world skills.

For bass and keyboard, I specialise in helping beginners get up and running quickly. You’ll learn practical techniques, how to play songs, and a solid foundation in music theory — without getting buried in unnecessary complexity.

For drums, I teach everything from complete beginners to advanced players, covering technique, timing, coordination, groove, fills and the skills needed to play confidently with other musicians.

What you can expect

  • Lessons built around the music you want to play
  • Clear, achievable goals for every stage
  • Supportive feedback that builds confidence
  • Flexibility for beginners and returning players
  • Guidance on playing with others and performing live
